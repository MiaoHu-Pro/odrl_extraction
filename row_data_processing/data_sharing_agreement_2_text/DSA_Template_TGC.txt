2. DATA PROTECTION
2.1 Nature of the Processing
2.1.1 The Parties acknowledge that the factual arrangements between them
dictate the role of each Party in respect of the Data Protection Laws.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, each Party agrees that the nature of the
Processing under this Agreement will be as follows:
(a) the Parties shall each Process the Personal Data;
(b) each Party shall act as a Controller in respect of the Processing of the
Personal Data on its own behalf as described in Appendix A.
2.2 Data Controller Obligations
2.2.1 Each Party shall in relation to the Processing of the Personal Data comply
with its respective obligations under the Data Protection Laws.
2.2.2 Without limiting the generality of the obligation set out in clause 2.1.1, in
particular, each Party shall:
(a) ensure that all fair processing notices have been given (and/or, as
applicable, consents obtained) and are sufficient in scope to enable each
Party to Process the Personal Data as required in order to use the
Personal Data for the Permitted Purpose;
(b) ensure that all Personal Data disclosed or transferred to, or accessed by,
the other Party is accurate, up-to-date and complete to enable either
Party to Process the Personal Data;
(c) work together to answer Data Subject Requests within 30 days and free
of charge to the Data Subject;
(d) ensure that appropriate technical and organisational security measures
are in place sufficient to comply with at least the obligations imposed
on the Controller by the Security Requirements and where requested
the each Party shall provide to the other evidence of its compliance with
such requirements;
(e) support the other Party to make any required notifications to the ICO
and/or other equivalent relevant regulator and affected Data Subjects;

reasonable changes to this Schedule to ensure that it addresses any change in the Data
Protection Laws in accordance with good market practice.
3 RETENTION AND DISPOSAL OF DATA
3.1 The Parties acknowledge that the Personal Data shall be retained in accordance with Data
Protection Laws and the Parties' respective retention policies and practices.
3.2 The Parties acknowledge and understand that each Party may wish retain some Personal Data
following termination of the Programme and/or the Principal Agreement. Each Party agrees
that it shall only do so where that Party may lawfully retain and use the relevant Personal
Data, and will ensure the relevant Data Subjects are informed of this continued use.
3.3 If any Personal Data are no longer required by a Party following termination or expiry of
the Principal Agreement, the Party shall securely destroy or delete such Personal Data as
soon as reasonably possible following termination or expiry of the Principal Agreement.
4 DIRECT MARKETING
4.1 If the Parties process Personal Data for the purposes of direct marketing, the Parties will
work together to ensure that:
4.1.1 the appropriate level of any necessary consents have been obtained from the relevant
Data Subjects to allow the Personal Data to be used for the purposes of direct
marketing in compliance with the Data Protection Laws (which may include ensuring
that any such consent names both Parties); and
4.1.2 effective procedures are in place to allow relevant Data Subjects to 'opt-out' from the
Parties using their Personal Data for such direct marketing purposes.

5. INSURANCE
5.1 Each Party agrees to obtain and keep in full force and effect at all times a policy or policies
of insurance which, in the Party’s reasonable view, covers its potential liabilities under this
Agreement and to deliver to the other Party upon request:
(a) copies of all applicable insurance policies taken out pursuant to the
provisions of this Agreement; and
(b) evidence of premiums paid in relation to such insurance.
6. POINTS OF CONTACT
6.1 Each Party shall appoint a single point of contact as set out in Appendix A who will work
together to reach resolution with regards to any issues arising from the data sharing
arrangement.
7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each Party will use reasonable endeavours to keep confidential and not to disclose to any
third party, other than its employees, officers and agents who reasonably need to receive
the information for the purposes of carrying out its obligations under this Agreement, any
information which is marked “confidential”, described as “confidential” or which, by its
nature or from the circumstances of its disclosure, should reasonably be presumed to be
confidential.
7.2 The obligation in clause 7.1 shall survive termination of this Agreement for five (5) years,
but will not apply to any information which:
7.2.1 was or becomes part of the public domain through no fault of the receiving
Party;
7.2.2 becomes available to the receiving Party by an unconnected third party with
the right to make such disclosure;
7.2.3 is approved in writing by the disclosing Party for release;
7.2.4 was independently developed by the disclosing Party; or
7.2.5 is required by law to be disclosed to any regulatory authority or court of
competent jurisdiction, or which is required to be disclosed pursuant to a
request under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.
8. TERM
8.1 This Agreement shall take effect on the last date of signature and shall remain in effect until
31st March 2020, or until terminated by either Party on three months’ notice in writing.

9. GENERAL
9.1 No Party shall use the other Party’s name or logo in any press release or product advertising,
or for any other commercial purpose, without the prior written consent of that Party.
9.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the
authorised representative of each Party.
9.3 No failure or delay by a Party to exercise any right or remedy provided under this
Agreement or by law shall constitute a waiver of that or any other right or remedy, nor shall
it prevent or restrict the further exercise of that or any other remedy. No single or partial
exercise of such right or remedy shall prevent or restrict the further exercise of that or any
other right or remedy.
9.4 A person who is not a Party to this Agreement shall have no rights under the Contracts
(Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 to enforce any term of this Agreement.
9.5 Nothing in this Agreement is intended to, or shall be deemed to, establish any partnership
or joint venture between any of the Parties, constitute any Party the agent of another Party,
or authorise any Party to make or enter into any commitments for or on behalf of any other
Party.
9.6 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supercedes and
extinguishes all previous agreements, promises, assurances, warranties, representations
and understandings between them, whether written or oral, relating to the subject matter.
10. LAW AND JURISDICTION
10.1 This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it or its subject
matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by
and construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales.
10.2 Each Party agrees that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to
settle any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or its subject
matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims).
