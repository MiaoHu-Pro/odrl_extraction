The purpose of this agreement is to establish the principles and practices for sharing the data from <AA> – <System A> with <BB> – <System B> for < (specify the purpose and objective of this agreement e.g. reporting and analytical purposes)>. 
Distinct use cases are outlined in section 6. Use Cases*.
* One agreement can have multiple use cases.
Scope
This document outlines the agreed conditions of data sharing between <AA> and <BB>.
This document does not review the specific methods of constructing the connection, or applying security controls.
This document does not imply legal obligations.
Definitions
Personal information: Information or an opinion (including information or an opinion forming part of a database and whether or not recorded in a material form) about an individual whose identity is apparent or can reasonably be ascertained from the information or opinion.
Refer to the UNSW Data Governance Centre for all other definitions.
