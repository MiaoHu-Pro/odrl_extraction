HUG 2: Data Sharing Agreement Introduction This Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) sets out the data sharing arrangements between the Partners in relation to HUG 2.
It covers personal data being shared by the Grant Recipient with the Authority and personal data shared by the Authority with the Grant Recipient.
HUG 2 is supported by grant funding to the Grant Recipient from the Authority in accordance with the MoU.
HUG 2 has two key aims: To deliver progress towards the fuel poverty target to improve as many D, E, F or G rated fuel poor homes as reasonably practicable to a minimum energy efficiency rating of C by the end of 2030, and an interim milestone of D by 2025.
To deliver progress towards the UK’s statutory target for net zero by 2050 and enable the delivery of the wider policy package to phase out high-carbon heating for homes off the mains gas grid this decade by growing supply chains and ensuring such policies do not act to the detriment of fuel poor households.
The Grant Recipient has committed to spend the Grant to deliver Eligible Measures to Eligible Households, using Eligible Installers.
Under paragraph 190 of the MoU, as far as it is possible to do so in accordance with the DPA and UK GDPR, the Grant Recipient has agreed to collect information for evaluation and reporting purposes (referred to below as “the Information”) in a way which: Allows it to share the Information with the Authority.
Allows the Authority to share the Information with any of its research or evaluation partners; Allows the Authority to use the Information for research and statistical purposes (this does not include publishing the Information in a way that identifies individual households) provided always that the Authority complies with the provisions of the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR; and Allows the Authority to use the Information for official communications and audit activities.
Under paragraph 113 of the MoU, the Grant Recipient will provide a monthly report to the Authority.
The KPI data will cover activity from the first day of the month to the last and will need to be provided on or before the 10th working day of the following month.
The Monthly Report shall detail monthly progress against KPIs stipulated in the Proposal, the top 5 risks and issues and any incidents of fraud, and an overall delivery confidence rating.
There is also space to highlight any items the Grant Recipient wishes to escalate for attention by the Authority.
The Grant Recipient will report to the Authority on the performance of the project(s) set out in their Proposal, in line with the stated KPIs HUG 2: Data Sharing Agreement using a Monthly Report online reporting platform.

Freedom of Information Requests Partners subject to the requirements of FoIA and the Environmental Information Regulations 2006, will assist and cooperate with each other, to enable each to comply with its information disclosure obligations.
Where a request for information under FoIA or the Environmental Information Regulations 2006 is received by a Partner to this DSA, which relates to data that has been provided by another Partner under this DSA, the Partner receiving the request will take reasonable steps, where appropriate, to give the other Partner advance notice to allow it the opportunity to make representations on the potential impact of disclosure, or failing that, to draw the disclosure to the other Partner's attention after any such disclosure.
Each Partner shall be responsible for determining in its absolute discretion whether any information is exempt from disclosure in accordance with the provisions of the FoIA or the Environmental Information Regulations 2006.
Personal data breaches The Partners will follow their own internal processes on the discovery of a personal data breach and advise their own security teams.
In addition, each Partner will notify the other Partner of any personal data breach that relates to this data share, via direct contact with the project leads named in Appendix B of this DSA within 72 hours of discovering the personal data breach.
In the event of a personal data breach (or where there is reasonable cause to believe that such an incident may arise), the Partners will delay data transfers until the cause or incident is resolved, as authorised by the Authority’s Information Asset Owner and the Grant Recipient’s equivalent.
If the breach cannot be resolved or if - in the view of the Partners – it is very serious, data transfers will stop and will not resume until the Authority Informational Asset Owner and the Grant Recipient’s equivalent are satisfied with the security arrangements.
HUG 2: Data Sharing Agreement Any Partner who decides that a personal data breach that affects, or is relevant to, the processing under this DSA must be self-reported to the ICO shall ensure that the other Partner is notified of this.
Dispute Resolution Disputes between the Partners regarding the operation of this DSA will be resolved in the following way: In the first instance a material breach will be reported between the project leads on each side, named in Appendix B of this DSA.
An assessment by the breaching party will be conducted promptly to identify if the breach is ongoing or was a one-off, and the potential impact of the breach.
All material breaches will be notified to the Data Protection Officers in the Authority and the Grant Recipient.
The outcomes of the assessment conducted by the project leads on each side, named in Appendix B of this DSA, will be discussed and actions identified.
Review Scheduled formal reviews of this DSA are not expected to take place to assess the ongoing effectiveness of this data sharing initiative and this DSA.
This DSA will only be reviewed if the purpose of the processing changes, or the processing otherwise changes in a way that affects the rights of data subjects.
Termination Any Partner can terminate this DSA, without giving a reason, on expiry of one (1) month’s written notice to the others.
Notice of termination would also trigger a formal review of the HUG 2 grant funding provided to the Grant Recipient.
Any Partner can terminate this DSA with immediate effect, where another Partner materially breaches any of its obligations to this DSA.
Termination notices should be addressed to the Information Asset Owners at the Authority and the Grant Recipient.
In the event of termination, data will cease to be shared under the terms of this DSA.

Nature and purposes of the processing The Grant Recipient will share with the Authority personal data relating to delivery of HUG 2.
The Authority will process the data, including matching it with other datasets and onward sharing with other parties, to effectively manage and review the use of public funds and to support further research, evaluation, and statistical reporting.
