2.
Background
2.1. The Sending Controller(s) agree to share the Personal Data with the Receiving
Controller in the European Economic Area (EEA) on Terms set out in the Agreement
and each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule for which they are a party.
2.2. The Receiving Controllers each agree to use the Personal Data within the EEA on the
Terms set out in this Agreement and each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule
for which they are a party.
2.3. The Sending Controller and Receiving Controller acknowledge that data sharing
under this arrangement is a Controller to Controller arrangement, therefore each
organisation is independently responsible for meeting its Controller responsibilities as
set out in Data Protection Legislation.
2.4. The Receiving Controller acknowledges that for any information it receives under this
Agreement and each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule, it will be the sole
Controller of the information it has received but shall have no rights or influence over
the data source from where the transmitted information originated.
2.5. The body of the text of this agreement must be used with a Purpose Specific Data
Sharing Schedule.

4.
Purpose
4.1. The United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and United
Kingdom Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA18) require all organisations to follow a
framework for effective, secure and lawful data sharing. This Agreement sets out the
framework for the sharing of Personal Data between the Parties as Controllers. It
defines the principles and procedures that the Parties shall adhere to and the
responsibilities the Parties owe to each other and Data Subjects.
4.2. This Data Sharing Agreement consists of a unified data sharing Agreement and a
number of Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedules for specific governance
arrangements, confirming roles, responsibilities and accountabilities of involved
health and care partners in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule.
4.3. The Parties agree to only process Personal Data, as described in each Purpose
Specific Data Sharing Schedule. The Parties shall not Process Personal Data which
is shared under this Agreement in a way that is incompatible with the purposes
described in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule (the Agreed Purpose).
4.4. Each party shall appoint a single point of contact who will work together with the other
Parties to reach an agreement with regards to any issues arising from the data sharing
and to actively improve the effectiveness of the data sharing initiative. The points of
contact for each of the Parties are documented in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedule.

Requirements at all times during the Term of this Agreement.

the Term of this Agreement are documented in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedule, together with any access and Processing restrictions as agreed and
established by the Parties.
6.2. The Shared Personal Data must not be irrelevant or excessive with regard to the
Agreed Purposes.

7.
Fair and lawful processing
7.1. Each party shall ensure that it Processes the transferred Personal or Special
Categories Data fairly and lawfully in accordance with clause 7.2 during the Term of
this Agreement.
7.2. Each party shall ensure that it identifies the relevant lawful basis for the transfer of
any Personal or Special Categories Data within each Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedule and any ongoing Processing.
7.3. Each party must meet the requirements of UK GDPR Article 5, Data Protection
Principles relating to Processing Personal Data within each Purpose Specific Data
Sharing Schedule.
7.4. Data Protection Principles, UK GDPR Article 5:
(a) Lawfully, fairly and in a transparent manner
(b) Purpose Limitation
(c) Data minimisation
(d) Accurately
(e) Storage limitation
(f) Integrity and confidentiality
(g) Accountability
7.5. Each Sending Controller shall, in respect of transmitted Personal Data, ensure that
their Privacy Notices are clear and provide sufficient information to the Data Subjects
for them to understand what of their Personal Data the Sending Controller is sharing
with the Receiving Controller, the circumstances in which it will be shared, the
purposes for the data sharing and either the identity of the Receiving Controller or a
description of the type of organisations that will receive the Personal Data.
8.
Data quality
8.1. The Parties must develop a reliable means of converting Transferred Personal Data
to ensure compatibility with each party's respective datasets as set out within each
Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule.
8.2. Each Sending Controller shall, in so far as is reasonable, ensure that before data is
transmitted, Personal Data is accurate and that it has appropriate internal procedures
in place for the Receiving Controller to sample information intended to be transmitted.
8.3. Transferred Personal Data must be limited to the Personal Data described within the
relevant Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule of this Agreement.
9.

about the Processing of their Personal Data through an Information Rights Request,
covering:

9.5. The Parties agree to provide reasonable assistance as is necessary to each other to
enable them to comply with Information Rights Requests and to respond to any other
queries or complaints from Data Subjects.
9.6. Where the Sending Controller is transmitting information to support a secondary use
it is the sending organisations responsibility to ensure the national data opt-out has
been applied.
9.7. Where the Receiving Controller receives data from the Sending Controller under an
Agreed Purpose and lawful basis where the national data opt-out does not apply, it
shall be the Receiving Controllers responsibility to apply the national data opt-out on
any secondary use Processing they undertake and to ensure that Processing is not
contrary to the agreement set out in the Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule nor
Data Protection legislation.
10.
Data retention and deletion
10.1. The Receiving Controller shall not retain nor process Transmitted Personal Data for
longer than is necessary to carry out the Agreed Purposes described in each Purpose
Specific Data Sharing Schedule.
10.2. Notwithstanding clause 10.1, the Parties shall continue to retain Transferred Personal
Data in accordance with any statutory or professional retention periods applicable to
them.
10.3. The Controllers shall follow the Records Management Code of Practice for Health and
Social Care 2016 (or any superseding code of practice) in the event information is
retained.
10.4. Each Receiving Controller may retain any transmitted personal data where specified
in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule.

10.5. Where clause 10.4 is not engaged, each Receiving Controller shall ensure that the
data is securely deleted in the following circumstances:
(a) on termination of the Agreement or Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedule;
(b) on expiry of the Term of the Agreement;
(c) once Processing of the Transferred Personal Data is no longer
necessary for the purposes it was originally shared for.
10.6. Following the deletion of Transferred Personal Data in accordance with clause 10.5,
each Receiving Controller shall notify the relevant Sending Controller that the
Transferred Personal Data in question has been securely deleted.
11.
Transfers
11.1. For the purposes of this clause, Third Party Processing shall mean any Processing of
Personal Data transmitted under any Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule
between a Receiving Controller and a Third Party.
11.2. A Receiving Controller shall not disclose nor transfer any information it receives under
agreements in the Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedules to a Controller or
processor located outside the European Economic Area (EEA) without the written
consent of the Sending Controller.
11.3. Clause 11.2 will not apply to any data transfers carried out by the Sending Controller
in respect of Transferred Personal Data detailed in this Agreements Purpose Specific
Data Sharing Schedules.
12.
Security and training
12.1. Each Sending Controller shall only provide the transmitted Personal Data to the
Receiving Controller by using proportionately secured measures.
12.2. Having regard to the state of technological development and the cost of implementing
such measures, the Parties have in place appropriate technical and organisational
security measures in order to prevent:
(a) unauthorised or unlawful Processing of transmitted Personal Data; and,
(b) the accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to, the transmitted
Personal Data,
(c) ensure a level of security appropriate to:
(d) the harm that might result from such unauthorised or unlawful
Processing or accidental loss, alteration, destruction or damage to
transmitted Personal Data; and
(e) the nature of the transmitted Personal Data to be protected.
12.3. It is the responsibility of each party to ensure that its Staff members are appropriately
trained to handle and process the Transferred Personal Data in accordance with
appropriate technical and organisational security measures together with any other
applicable Privacy and Data Protection Requirements.

12.4. The level, content and regularity of training referred to in clause 12.3 shall be

13.
Data security breaches and reporting procedures
13.1. Having considered the applicable Privacy and Data Protection Requirements, the
Parties have in place their own guidance and processes that must be followed in the
event of a Data Security Breach.
13.2. Parties are under a strict obligation to notify any potential or actual losses of the
Transferred Personal Data to Parties to each Purpose Specific Data Sharing Schedule
as soon as possible and, in any event, within one (1) Working Day of identification of
any potential or actual loss to enable the Parties to consider what action is required
in order to resolve the issue in accordance with the applicable national data protection
laws and guidance.
13.3. Clause 13.1 also applies to any breaches of data integrity.
13.4. The Parties agree to provide reasonable assistance as is necessary to each other to
facilitate the handling of any instances under clause 13.1 or 13.3 breach in an
expeditious and compliant manner.
14.
Review and termination of Agreement
14.1. New Parties can only be added in line with the relevant Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedules change management process.
14.2. In the event that a party terminates this Agreement or a new Controller joins the
Agreement in accordance with clause 14.1, an amended and updated version of this
Agreement will be circulated in line with the relevant Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedules change management process.
14.3. The Parties shall review the effectiveness of the Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedules of this Agreement as set out in each Purpose Specific Data Sharing
Schedule. The Parties shall continue, amend or terminate the Agreement depending
on the outcome of this review.
14.4. The review of the effectiveness of this Agreement will involve assessing:
(a) Whether the purposes for which the Transferred Personal Data is being
processed are still the ones listed in each Purpose Specific Data
Sharing Schedule of this Agreement;
(b) Whether the Transferred Personal Data is still as listed in each Purpose
Specific Data Sharing Schedule of this Agreement;
(c) Whether the legal framework governing data quality, retention, and
Data Subjects' rights are being complied with; and,
(d) Whether Personal Data breaches involving the Transferred Personal
Data have been handled in accordance with this Agreement and data
protection legislation.

other arbitration, mediation or other dispute resolution proceedings developed for data
protection disputes.
