BACKGROUND
(A) The Parties wish to enter into a data sharing framework agreement to create a
framework of terms and conditions that will apply on each and every occasion one
Party wishes to transfer Shared Personal Data to the other Party.
(B) This is a free-standing Agreement that does not incorporate commercial business
terms established by the Parties under separate commercial arrangements.
(C) The terms and conditions of this Agreement will govern the relationship between the
Parties in respect of Shared Personal Data and provide a mechanism whereby the
Parties may enter into data sharing arrangements in response to a public heath
incident or event in relation to communicable disease and other risks to public health
through the execution of a Data Sharing Addendum (DSA).
(D) This Agreement including associated DSAs will ensure all Parties can carry out their
statutory functions in line with Regulation 3 of The Health Service (Control of Patient
Information) Regulations 2002 in a lawful, auditable and transparent manner.
(E) The Data Receiver agrees to use the Personal Data within the EEA on the terms set
out in this Agreement.

2. Purpose
2.1 This Agreement sets out the framework for the sharing of Personal Data when one
Controller (the Data Discloser) discloses Personal Data to another Controller (the
Data Receiver). It defines the principles and procedures that the Parties shall adhere
to and the responsibilities the Parties owe to each other.
2.2 The Parties consider this data sharing necessary to fulfil their statutory duties in
relation to public health and communicable disease surveillance.
2.3 Each time the Data Receiver is engaged to receive Shared Personal Data, a DSA shall
be completed. The Parties shall assist each other to discuss and agree each DSA and
both Data Receiver and Data Discloser shall sign the DSA once it has been agreed.
Upon the execution of each DSA the terms and conditions agreed in the DSA shall be
incorporated into this Agreement and shall not form a separate contract to it.
2.4 The Parties shall not process Shared Personal Data in a way that is incompatible with
the purposes described in this Clause 2 and the DSA (Agreed Purpose).
2.5 Each Party shall appoint a single point of contact (SPoC) and delegated deputy who
will work together to reach an agreement with regards to any issues arising from the
data sharing and to actively improve the effectiveness of the data sharing initiative.
The points of contact for each of the Parties are:
(a) , Head of Information Governance & Privacy, UKHSA
(b) , Public Health Intelligence Lead, London Borough of
Camden

commence, covers the intended data sharing pursuant to this Agreement, unless an
exemption applies.

4.2 The Shared Personal Data must not be irrelevant or excessive with regard to the
Agreed Purpose.

5. Lawful, fair and transparent processing
5.1 Each Party shall ensure that it processes the Shared Personal Data fairly and lawfully
during the Term of this Agreement.
5.2 Each Party shall ensure that it has legitimate grounds under the Data Protection
Legislation for the processing of Shared Personal Data.
5.3 Each Party agrees that the legitimate grounds under the Data Protection Legislation
for the processing of Shared Personal Data are:
(a) UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e) – the processing is necessary for the performance
of the Parties’ official tasks carried out in the public interest.
(b) UK GDPR Article 9(2)(i) – the processing is necessary for reasons of public
interest in the area of public health.
(c) Data Protection Act 2018 – Schedule 1, Part 1 (3) – ‘Public Health’
6. Data quality
6.1 The Data Discloser shall take all reasonable steps to ensure that the Shared Personal
Data are accurate.
6.2 Shared Personal Data must be limited to the Personal Data described in each
associated fully executed DSA to this Agreement.
7. Data Subjects' rights
7.1 The Parties each agree to provide such assistance as is reasonably required to enable

relating to the request. The SPoC for each Party are detailed in clause 2.5.

8.2 Notwithstanding clause 8.1, the Parties shall continue to retain Shared Personal Data
in accordance with any statutory or professional retention periods applicable in their
respective areas of work and profession.

Schedule 4 Deletion Procedure
A data destruction certificate must be used to notify the Data Discloser of the destruction of
the Shared Personal Data.
The terms of this Agreement require confirmation that all the physical and logical data detailed
on a data destruction certificate has been destroyed, including the original data and any copies
in whole or in part.
The following standards and guidelines are the minimum basis for data decommissioning or
destruction:
National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) guidance on end-user device reset procedures:
https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/end-user-device-guidance-factory-reset-and-
reprovisioning
NCSC guidance on secure sanitisation of storage media:
https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/secure-sanitisation-storage-media
